# Terms and Conditions Draft

This is product/legal draft copy for Playbook. Review with counsel before
publishing or sending to institutional users.

## Service Use

Playbook provides chat-first support for athletics department processes, NIL,
compliance, knowledge-base questions, and admin analytics. Users must use the
service only for authorized department workflows and must not attempt to bypass
role access, rate limits, security controls, or audit logging.

## No Professional Advice

Playbook may provide procedural guidance grounded in department-approved sources.
It does not provide legal, medical, mental-health, emergency, or financial
advice. Users should contact the appropriate department office or emergency
service when a request falls outside supported guidance.

## User Content

Users are responsible for the files, questions, and other content they submit.
Do not upload secrets, credentials, payment data, protected medical information,
or information that is not authorized for Playbook processing.

## Essential Cookies and Similar Technologies

Playbook uses only essential cookies and similar browser storage by default.
These are required to provide the service, keep users signed in, protect OAuth
login state, maintain session security, enforce abuse controls, and support
basic service operation.

Playbook does not use analytics cookies, marketing cookies, advertising pixels,
session replay tools, or cross-site tracking cookies unless a separate notice and
consent or preference mechanism is implemented first.

If essential cookies are blocked, parts of Playbook may not work, including
login, session renewal, secure workspace access, and streamed chat responses.

## Acceptable Use

Users must not:

- attempt unauthorized access to another user's conversations, files, analytics,
  or admin tools,
- upload malware, exploit payloads, or content intended to attack the service,
- use automated traffic to overload chat, upload, retrieval, or admin insight
  workflows,
- extract, disclose, or misuse another person's confidential or personal data,
- use Playbook to generate or facilitate harassment, threats, illegal activity,
  or policy violations.

## Availability and Changes

Playbook may change, suspend, or limit features to protect security, reliability,
privacy, compliance, or system cost controls. Maintenance windows, rate limits,
and usage limits may apply.

## Contact

Production launch requires a published support, privacy, security, and incident
contact before real users are onboarded.
