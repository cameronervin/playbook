# ADR 0001 — ADR Template

> Copy this file to start a new ADR. **Number ADRs sequentially**
> (`0001`, `0002`, `0003`, …) and use a short kebab-case slug for the filename,
> e.g. `0003-async-task-queue.md`. One decision per ADR. Never renumber or
> delete an accepted ADR — supersede it with a new one instead.

## Status

One of: `Proposed` · `Accepted` · `Superseded by ADR-XXXX` · `Deprecated`

## Context

What is the problem or force that prompted this decision? Capture the relevant
constraints, requirements, and options considered. Keep it factual — describe
the situation as it was when the decision was made.

## Decision

The decision that was made, stated plainly in the active voice
("We will …"). Be specific enough that a reader can act on it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result? Include:

- **Positive** — benefits gained.
- **Negative** — costs, trade-offs, new constraints.
- **Follow-ups** — work this decision creates or unblocks.
